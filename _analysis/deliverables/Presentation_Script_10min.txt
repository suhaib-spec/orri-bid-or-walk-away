DELIVERY TIPS
------------------------------------------------------------
- Pause for one beat after every big number. Silence makes numbers land.
- Face the judges, not the screen. Point at one thing at a time.
- Lean on the three "hooks": the $220M trap (slide 1), the one-well risk (slide 2), "prove it and we'll pay" (slide 6).
- Honesty rules: the fund is an assumption; decline rates were set by judgement, not fitted curves; never mention anything after Aug 15, 2018.
- Running long? Drop the Ship Shoal and Stones lines on slide 3 (saves about 10 seconds). Hurricanes come up in Q&A: we cut output 2% a year for them.
