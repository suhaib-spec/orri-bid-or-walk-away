### 1.2 Chain of title. ORRI instrument (FACT: SourceDocs\5. GI 110 and GI 116 ORRI ASSIGNMENT.pdf)
- **p1–2:** CNG cover letter to MMS, Apr 12, 1999. Filed as a **non-required filing (NRF), record only**. It requests an effective date of Mar 1, 1998. The letter names the assignees as only CNG and BHP Petroleum (Deepwater) and **omits Amoco**, a discrepancy with the instrument. The covered leases are OCS-G 18069 (GI 111), 13944 (GI 116) and 13943 (GI 110).
- **p3:** "Assignment of Overriding Royalty Interest".
  - **Assignors:** Anadarko Petroleum Corp. and Shell Offshore Inc., who "now own all right, title and interest" in the three leases.
  - **Assignees:** CNG Producing Co., BHP Petroleum (Deepwater) Inc. and **Amoco Production Company**.
  - **Grant:** "an overriding royalty interest of **one percent (1%) of six-sixths (6/6ths)** proportionately divided among said Assignees, in accordance with each of the Assignees previously owned working interest in **Grand Isle Block 110**".
  - **Seafloor to and including 13,000' subsea:** CNG 75%, BHP 25%.
  - **Below 13,000':** CNG 25%, BHP 25%, **Amoco 50%**.
  - It applies to all three leases "insofar and only insofar as" they cover the described depths and lands. The leases are described as All of the 5,000-acre blocks.
- **p4 terms:**
  - Assignee cannot take in kind; value is the price realized by Assignor, computed at the well.
  - The ORRI is free of exploration, development, drilling and producing costs. It **bears its share of marketing costs** (dehydration, treating, compression, not exceeding what MMS allows on its royalty) and **its share of severance, ad valorem and other taxes**.
  - Assignor has free use of oil and gas for operations, so there is no royalty on fuel.
  - **No obligation** to maintain the leases, drill, or protect against drainage, and no liability for lease termination "however occurring".
  - There is no proportionate-reduction clause. The ORRI is of 6/6ths (= 8/8ths, the whole) of each lease.
  - No warranty.
  - **Executed Oct 20, 1998; effective Mar 1, 1998.** That is the same effective date as the unit.
- **p5:** Acknowledgments. Anadarko: Harris Co., TX, Oct 20, 1998. Shell: Orleans Parish, Jan 7, 1999. The Louisiana notary's "commission expires: At Death" is normal for LA (lifetime commission), so it is not a defect.

**Derivation (FACT + INF).** Amoco's share = 1% × 50% = **0.005** per lease, below 13,000' subsea only.
- **Unit-level net = 0.005 × (TP110 + TP116) = 0.005 × 100% = 0.005 of unit production.**
- This matches Attachment A's 0.005 for each of GI 110 and GI 116, provided all production is from below 13,000' subsea.
- The ORRI also burdens GI 111 (OCS-G 18069), which is not in Attachment A. That lease was eliminated from the unit on 12/27/2005 because it was not receiving an allocation, and its expiration date was 6/30/2002 per Exhibit B. **INF:** it is likely expired and worthless.
- Amoco's origin: per the GI 110 serial register, Amoco held **operating rights only, 50%, 13,000' subsea and below** (eff 5/4/1995). Those rights passed to Anadarko/Shell by 3/1/1998. The ORRI was the retained consideration.
