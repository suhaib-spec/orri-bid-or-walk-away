**Checks (computed):**
- The "Totals" row is cumulative volume, not a rate. Σ liquids 127.3 kb/d-yr × 0.365 = 46.46 MMbbl. Σ gas 227.3 × 0.365 = 82.96 Bcf. Σ revenue = 3,313.0 exactly.

- **PV basis reverse-engineered:** mid-year discounting at 10% to 1/1/2018 reproduces both PVs exactly (Rem PV 1,836.8 and Total PV 2,273.9, with 2016–17 compounded forward). End-year discounting would give 1,751.3; start-of-year would give 1,926.5.
- Implied realised price = Gross Rev ÷ boe (6:1). It runs $53.6/boe in 2018, about $64–68 by 2026–2030, and about $100 in 2036–37, so WoodMac uses a nominal, escalating price deck.

## 2. Chain of title for the ORRI

### 2.1 Lessor → BP (FACT)
- **OCS-G 24102 (MC 727).** Lease Sale 182, effective 6/1/2002, ten-year primary term, original lessee BP E&P 100%. Royalty 12½%, all of Block 727, 5,760 ac, bonus $5,251,704 (SD1 p1–2). The Sale 182 addendum grants a deepwater **royalty suspension of 12 MMboe**, subject to oil and gas price thresholds (SD1 p2, p4–5).
- **OCS-G 24107 (MC 771).** Same sale and terms. Original lessee Dominion E&P 100%. Royalty 12½%, 12 MMboe RSV, bonus $4,155,500 (SD2 p2, p4).
- BP's deep operating rights reached **63.75%** in both leases at 17,500'–99,999': MC 727 NE/4+S/2 effective 10/1/2007 and 12/7/2007, with Eni 25% and Marubeni 11.25% (SD3 p2). MC 771 all-block 17,500'–99,999', effective 9/1/2007 and 12/7/2007 (SD4 p2).

### 2.2 The reserving instrument (FACT)
- **Assignment and Bill of Sale** from BP Exploration & Production Inc. (Assignor) to Deep Gulf Energy II, LLC (Assignee), dated **August 6, 2012**, effective **April 1, 2012**.
  - Executed by Kemper Howe (Attorney-in-Fact) for BP and Thomas E. Young for DGE II. Acknowledged in Harris County, TX, August 2012.
  - **Recorded** in Plaquemines Parish, LA, on 08/09/2012 at 12:22:01 PM, File No. 2012-00003677, Book 1275, Page 334, 14 pages (SD5 p1).
  - **Filed with BOEM** on 10/05/2012 as Exhibit "A" to Form BOEM-0151, Assignment of Operating Rights, OCS-G 24102, S/2 and NE/4 below 17,500' to 99,999' TVD, 63.75% BP → DGE II. **BOEM approved it on 5/31/2013** (SD6 p2–4).
- **Reservation clause** (SD5 p4; SD6 p6–7). Assignor "expressly except[s], reserve[s] and retain[s], unto itself, its affiliates, successors and assigns an overriding royalty interest equal to ten percent (10%) of all [Hydrocarbons] produced, saved and sold from those depths and portions of the leases described on Exhibit 'A', **proportionately reduced to the interests in the Properties being assigned to Purchaser (equating to a net overriding royalty interest of 6.375% assuming the interests specified on Exhibit 'A' are correct)**." Exhibit B (Excluded Properties) item (a) also reserves "The proportionately reduced ten percent (10%) overriding royalty interest" (SD5 p11; SD6 p13).
- **Other ORRI terms** (FACT, SD5 p4–5):
  - It is "over and above" all burdens of record at the Effective Time.
  - It is "based, paid and calculated in the same manner… as the lease royalty." **During royalty relief it is calculated as if there were no royalty relief.** The 12 MMboe RSV therefore does not reduce the ORRI.
  - It bears its proportionate share of excise, production and severance taxes.
  - It is free of all costs **except** costs lawfully deductible from the federal lease royalty. Transportation and processing allowances may therefore be deducted.
  - The Assignee has **no obligation** to maintain the leases, drill, or protect against drainage.
  - The Assignee may pool or unitise the ORRI without BP's joinder, but only into a unit that includes Exhibit A leases and **no other leases**.
  - Covenants run with the land (¶5). The PSA dated **July 2, 2012** is incorporated and **controls in any conflict** (¶6). **The PSA is not in the data room.**
- **No conditions found** (FACT, absence): no payout trigger, no conversion, no term or expiry date, no well-specific limitation, no "only after" clause. **There is also no extension/renewal (anti-washout) clause**, so the ORRI lives only as long as the two leases.

### 2.5 Depth and aliquot nuances (INFERENCE unless stated)
- **The ORRI covers deep rights only** (17,500'–99,999' TVD). This is co-extensive with the unitised interval, which is 17,500' TVD SS to 99,999' TVD SS (FACT, Unit Agmt p4 "Unitized Substances"; p21 plat). That fit is good.
- WoodMac expects the second well "**to be recompleted into a shallower gas-rich sand**." If that sand sits **above 17,500' TVD SS**:
  - In **MC 771** the ORRI would not apply. BP never owned shallow rights there; Eni 75% and Marubeni 25% operate them (SD4 p1, p3). The sand would also fall outside the unit.
  - In **MC 727** the result is arguable. Exhibit A line 1 conveys BP's 100% record title in NE/4+S/2 with no depth limit, so one could argue the ORRI also burdens shallow MC 727 rights, possibly at the full 10%. The "6.375%" parenthetical cuts against that reading. This is ambiguous and is not counted in the base value.
  - Most likely the sand is still below 17,500' TVD SS, given a subsalt middle-Miocene field with "deepest… frac packs in the GoM". **Diligence item:** confirm the TVD SS of every completion.
- The **"proportionately reduced to the interests … being assigned"** wording is anchored to what BP assigned (63.75%). It is not affected by later operating-rights reshuffles: Eni's 25% exit on 12/17/2013 went to DGE II and Marubeni (Marubeni from 11.25% to 16.875%), and Murphy came in. Those Eni-derived interests never carried BP's override, but the ORRI is still 6.375% of 8/8ths.
- **Serial-register clerical issues** (FACT, SD3 p3):
  - The record-title entry effective 4/1/2012 to DGE II describes the land as "**S1/2; CW1/4**". This is native PDF text, a BOEM typo for NE1/4.
  - "Unit suspension of production approved 6/1/2013 through **4/30/2013**" has an end date before its start date. SD4 p2 gives 04/30/2014.
  - Both are harmless but worth noting.
- **BOEM-filed copy:** the package contains the BOEM-0151 with the ABOS attached for **OCS-G 24102 only** (SD6). The parish-recorded copy covers both leases (SD5). The serial register shows DGE II's 63.75% in G 24107, effective 4/1/2012 and entered 24-May-2013 (SD4 p2), but the G 24107 BOEM filing itself is not provided. This is a curative or diligence request.

### 2.6 Unit tract participation and effective ORRI share
- **Unit (FACT):** Contract 754313008, "MC 727 Unit (Kodiak Unit)". BSEE approved it 5/31/2013, effective **2/1/2013** (Art. XVI). Unit Area = **MC 727 S/2 (2,880 ac, G 24102) + MC 771 N/2 (2,880 ac, G 24107)**, depth-limited 17,500'–99,999' TVD SS. Exhibit A plat and Exhibit B are at SD Unit p21–22. Initial unit operator: DGE II.
- **Tract participation factors: not in the data room (FACT).**
  - Art. 3.4 and Art. 11.1 say Exhibit "C" (participating-area schedule and allocation percentages) "**will be submitted**". No Exhibit C is attached.
  - Art. 12.1 allocates production "on a volumetric basis of oil and gas in place under original reservoir conditions" (net acre-feet), **not by acreage**.
  - The serial register shows how allocation actually worked (FACT, SD3 p3–5). MC 727 was "**Held by unit production, lease not receiving an allocation**" from 2013 through May 2017. It was "lease **receiving** an allocation" in June–July 2017 and again from Mar–May 2018. MC 727 was "Placed on continuous production on 01/17/2017", consistent with the early-2017 recompletion into a new zone. So the initial participating area was effectively **100% MC 771**, and it was later revised to include MC 727 acreage.
- **Effective ORRI share (computed):** both unit tracts lie entirely within ORRI-burdened lands and depths.
  - MC 727 S/2 is within "NE/4 and S/2" at 17,500'+.
  - MC 771 N/2 is within "all of MC 771" at 17,500'+.
  - Each tract therefore carries an identical 6.375% burden on production allocated to it:
    - Effective share = Σ_tract (TPF_i × 6.375%) = 6.375% × Σ TPF_i = **6.375% of 8/8ths unit production**, for *any* allocation.
    - Illustration (acreage 50/50): 0.5×6.375% + 0.5×6.375% = 6.375%.
    - Actual 2016 to mid-2017 (MC 771 = 100%): 1.0×6.375% = 6.375%.
- The ORRI on **MC 727 NE/4** and on **MC 771 S/2** (both outside the unit) has **no current production**. It is option value only, for example on a third well or a unit expansion.
- **Do not add the two Attachment A lines** (0.06375 + 0.06375 = 12.75%). That would double-count the same unit production.
- **Art. 13.1 auto-contraction (FACT):** a lease not receiving an allocation on the 3rd anniversary of the initial participating area is eliminated from the unit. That date would be about Feb 2019 if the PA started at first production in Feb 2016. MC 727 now receives an allocation, so the risk is low. Even if MC 727 dropped out, the ORRI owner's share would be unchanged, because the MC 771 burden is identical (INFERENCE).

## 5. Inconsistencies, tricks and risks

1. **Production outage hidden by WoodMac (FACT; major).**
   - BOEM shows production ceased 06/07/2017 and resumed 02/22/2018. WoodMac's 2017 average of 10.5 kb/d + 25.2 mmcfd and its "peak 20,000 boe/d mid-2017" are inconsistent with roughly 5 months of 2017 production.
   - WoodMac's 2018 of 17.0 kb/d assumes a full year plus a 1H 2018 second well. Production was actually off for 52 days (14%) of early 2018, and there is no evidence the second well is online.
   - **WoodMac's 2018–2019 volumes are likely optimistic. Risk them.**
2. **Single-well concentration and mechanical risk (FACT/INFERENCE).**
   - All production to date comes from one HPHT well with record frac packs, and it has already had one 8.5-month failure.
   - The second well is unconfirmed: "drilled mid-2018" vs "onstream 1H 2018".
   - WoodMac flags downside from compartmentalisation and a weak drive mechanism.
   - Host dependency: a subsea tie-back to Eni-operated Devils Tower spar (INFERENCE: host uptime and cessation-of-production risk).
3. **Lease-continuity and anti-washout risk.** See §3: a 260-day gap with no SOP recorded, and an ORRI with no extension/renewal protection.
4. **Double-count trap.** Attachment A lists MC 727 and MC 771 separately at 0.06375 each. The correct field-level burden is **6.375% of unit 8/8ths**, not 12.75%.
5. **Excluded aliquots.**
   - The MC 727 NW/4 is not covered (Tubular Bells).
   - The MC 727 NE/4 and MC 771 S/2 are covered by the ORRI but lie outside the unit, so they produce nothing now.
   - The unit (S/2 of 727 + N/2 of 771) is fully covered.
6. **Depth limit.** The ORRI covers 17,500'–99,999' TVD only. Any shallower recompletion in MC 771 (WoodMac's "shallower gas-rich sand") would fall outside it. MC 727 shallow coverage is ambiguous (record-title line in Exhibit A).
7. **Deductions.** The ORRI is net of costs deductible from federal royalty (transportation, processing). WoodMac's "Gross Revenue" is pre-deduction, so ORRI revenue = 6.375% × gross revenue overstates cash slightly.
8. **Royalty relief is a positive.** The ORRI is computed as if there were no RSV, so the 12 MMboe suspension on each lease does not reduce it.
9. **PSA controls.** The July 2, 2012 PSA is incorporated and prevails in any conflict, but it is not provided. Request it to confirm there is no reassignment, conversion or buy-back right.
10. **WoodMac internal errors** (start date, second-well timing, Murphy effective date, operator entity). See §1.6.
11. **Record-title typo** "S1/2; CW1/4" and the inverted SOP dates in BOEM's register. Clerical only.
12. **No ORRI on the serial register.** This is normal for BOEM. Notice to third parties rests on the parish recordation (2012-00003677) and on BOEM's non-required filing of the ABOS with the OR assignment.
