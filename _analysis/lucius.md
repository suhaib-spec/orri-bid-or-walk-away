# LUCIUS (Keathley Canyon KC 874 / KC 875): Title, Unit and Economics Findings

Scope: the Lucius folder (`Lucius\Lucius (KC 875).pdf` plus the 20 files in `Lucius\SourceDocs\`). Every SourceDoc was read through its OCR text, and the key pages were also rendered and checked by eye. Where text was missing or garbled (the Unocal ORRI assignment, Teikoku, the leases, all Exhibit A/B/C tables and the Devon to BP Exhibit A), the page images were read directly.
Labels: **FACT** means stated in a document (file and page are cited). **INFERENCE** means my analysis. **HINDSIGHT** means the information is dated after 15 Aug 2018 and must NOT be used in the valuation.

## 0. Bottom line (decision-relevant)

1. **Attachment A's "0.02" for each of KC 874 and KC 875 is correct as a lease-level ORRI (2% of 8/8). It is NOT BP's share of Lucius field production.** Lucius is a BSEE-approved federal unit (Contract No. 754318001, approved 21 May 2018, **effective 1 Jan 2018**). Unit production is allocated to leases by Exhibit C: KC 874 (SE/4) **24.77%**, KC 875 (S/2) **46.67%**, KC 918 10.39%, KC 919 18.17%. BP's ORRI is a lease burden, so it attaches only to the production allocated to its leases.
   - Effective share of unit production = 0.02 x 24.77% + 0.02 x 46.67% = 0.004954 + 0.009334 = **0.014288 (1.4288%)**.
   - Someone who applies "0.02 + 0.02 = 4%" to the WoodMac field profile overstates Lucius volumes and value by **2.80x**. Someone who applies 2% to the whole field overstates by 1.40x. **This is the main trick in this folder.**
2. **The chain of title for KC 875 is documented. The instrument that created the KC 874 ORRI is not in the data room.** The 2% KC 874 override (surface to 23,000') appears only in the Devon to BP Exhibit A and in recitals in third-party instruments (Teikoku 2012, Exxon 2015, the Anadarko 2018 OA memo). Its terms cannot be verified: cost-free status, proportionate reduction, whether it is payable during royalty suspension, and the depth datum.
3. **WoodMac is stale or inconsistent on several points.** It says the blocks "have not been awarded a Federal Unit, and one is not expected", but the unit was approved 5/21/2018, about three weeks before the 6/12/2018 download. It also swaps INPEX and Eni in the participant summary and has internal well-count errors. WoodMac "Gross Revenue" includes **processing tariffs from Buckskin and Hadrian North tie-backs**, which an ORRI does not share. Price the ORRI volumes independently.
4. **Out-of-bounds document:** `79187.pdf` is a July 2019 BOEM filing (an ORRI assignment from Kayes to his revocable trust, effective 10 May 2019). It is **HINDSIGHT**: exclude it. It does not concern BP's override in any case.
5. **Reserve and execution risk (from WoodMac):** the forecast assumes a 9th well in Q4 2018 (about **42 MMboe** of the remaining reserves depends on it) and late-life sidetracks in 2025-27. Water cut is rising in several key wells.

### 2.4 Does the record support Attachment A's 0.02 on each block?
- **KC 875: yes.** 2% of 8/8, cost-free, not reducible below 2%, payable even when lessor royalty is suspended. **FACT.**
- **KC 874: yes on its face** (Devon to BP Exhibit A and four consistent third-party recitals). **But the creating instrument and its terms are missing.** Diligence item: ask the seller for the Devon to Anadarko KC 874 assignment or ORRI reservation, and for Farmout Arts. 3.5 and 4.2.
- **Lease-level vs unit-level (key point):** the ORRI burdens the *lease* ("produced and saved and marketed from OCS-G 21444"). It is computed "in the same manner as royalties ... to the lessor", and since 1/1/2018 lessor royalty on unitized substances is paid on **production allocated to each lease** (Unit Agreement Art. 12.1: allocation "regardless of where any well is drilled"). So:

```
Effective unit share = sum over BP's leases of (ORRI x lease allocation %)
KC 874: 0.02 x 0.2477 = 0.004954
KC 875: 0.02 x 0.4667 = 0.009334
TOTAL              = 0.014288 = 1.4288% of gross Lucius unit production (8/8)
Naive "0.02+0.02 on the field" = 0.04  ->  overstatement factor 0.04 / 0.014288 = 2.80x
```
(INFERENCE, standard OCS practice. The "in no event proportionately reduced below 2%" clause protects against WI-fraction reduction. It does not stop federal unit allocation from deciding which production counts as "from the Lease".)

### 2.5 Timing anomaly in the KC 875 chain (INFERENCE; low risk but flag it)
Devon conveyed to BP (effective 1/1/2010, signed April 2010, filed 16 Jul 2010) **before** Devon itself received the recorded KC 875 ORRI assignment (signed June to Sep 2010, filed Oct 2010). BP's title therefore rests on Devon's contractual right under the farmout plus after-acquired-title principles. The data room has no confirmatory Devon to BP re-conveyance after Oct 2010. Later industry instruments (2012, 2015, 2018) all recognise BP as the 2% owner.

Record owner is **BP Exploration & Production Inc.** (Delaware corp.). The FCX/Anadarko 2017 footnotes say "BP Exploration and Production Company **LP**". Confirm that the selling entity matches record title.

## 6. Inconsistencies, surprises, "tricks" and diligence items
1. **Unit dilution (main trick).** Attachment A shows 0.02 per block. The economic entitlement is 1.4288% of unit production (0.4954% via KC 874 plus 0.9334% via KC 875). If the seller allocated price equally between the two blocks, the allocation is also off: KC 875 carries about 65% of Lucius ORRI value and KC 874 about 35%.
2. **WoodMac says there is no federal unit and none is expected.** That is false as of the data-room date (approved 5/21/2018, effective 1/1/2018). A team relying on WoodMac might use well-location allocation or blended block shares.
3. **KC 874 ORRI creating instrument missing.** Terms unverified. The 2% figure is supported only by Exhibit A and recitals.
4. **Depth limit / datum.** The KC 874 ORRI covers "surf. to 23,000'" (no datum). Operating rights are split at 23,000' TVD. The unit is limited to 23,000' TVDSS. The gap between TVD and TVDSS (rig-floor elevation, roughly 80-100 ft) is immaterial in practice (INFERENCE). Any production below 23,000' would fall outside both the unit and the KC 874 ORRI. The KC 875 ORRI has no depth limit, but its unit allocation covers only to 23,000' TVDSS.
5. **Tract-factor redetermination risk.** Exhibit C can be revised prospectively (Art. 11.2/11.4). BP's overrides have no contractual protection or adjustment mechanism. Note the volumetric numbers: KC 874 SE/4 = 24.77%, while KC 874 #1 produced about 20 kb/d of the field's roughly 60-74 kb/d.
6. **Pre-1/1/2018 production not allocated under the unit** (Art. 12.1). Valuation begins at the 1/1/2018 effective date, so this is not an issue, but any historic ORRI statements would follow a different basis (pre-unit, per-lease well production or the private UPA). Don't benchmark against 2015-2017 ORRI receipts.
7. **Royalty relief.** It doesn't suspend the KC 875 ORRI (explicit clause). The KC 874 position is unknown.
8. **Cost-free vs "same manner as lessor royalty".** The KC 875 ORRI is "free of all costs ... transporting and delivering" but computed "in the same manner as royalties ... to the lessor". Federal royalty valuation allows transportation allowances, so there is a potential argument for netting transport. Assume small transport deductions as a downside case.
9. **No anti-washout / lease-termination survival.** If a lease terminates (late life), the ORRI dies with it. No re-lease protection. Leases are currently held by unit production.
10. **No payout, conversion or product-specific limits** in the KC 875 ORRI. It covers oil, gas and other liquid or gaseous hydrocarbons "produced and saved and marketed". There is no consent requirement to assign it, and no preferential right in the ORRI instrument itself.
11. **Preferential rights / AMI exposure.** The Lucius JOA (2010), UOA (2011) and Unit OA (2017, filed 2018) memos grant **preferential rights to purchase among WI parties**. BP is not a party, and ORRIs normally fall outside JOA prefs (INFERENCE). However, Devon to BP was made **"expressly subject to" the Keathley Canyon Operating Agreement between Unocal and Ocean Energy dated 20 Dec 2000**, which is not in the data room and could contain AMI or pref terms. Diligence item.
12. **BP retains 50% of KC 874 deep rights (below 23,000' TVD).** These are not offered. They can't conflict with the unit (limited to 23,000') but show the seller still has exposure on the block.
13. **Title timing gap.** Devon sold to BP before the KC 875 ORRI was formally conveyed to Devon (section 2.5).
14. **Entity name.** "BP Exploration & Production Inc." vs "BP Exploration and Production Company LP" in the 2017 recitals.
15. **WoodMac internal errors.** INPEX and Eni swapped; well counts (3 + 4 ≠ 8; "KC 919 #7" vs "KC 919 #1ST2"); "January 2017" water cut. The reserve case also relies on the 9th well (42 MMboe), and revenue includes tie-back tariffs (not ORRI-able).
16. **Duplicate file.** `5. ORRI ABOS Devon to BP.PDF` and `Lucius-DevonToBP-AST 62528.pdf` are the same BOEM-filed instrument (62528). There is only one conveyance, not two.
17. **Package cross-check.** The Devon to BP Exhibit A also covers **MC 110 1.3125% of 100%**, which matches Orion MC 110 0.013125 in Attachment A, and **WR 199 1% of 100%** (not in Attachment A: either excluded from the sale or a separate asset). Flag WR 199 to the package lead.

## 7. Net production series for the Lucius ORRI package (INFERENCE / calculation)

Net revenue uses WoodMac gross revenue x 0.014288. That is an **upper bound**, because WoodMac revenue may include tie-back processing tariffs. Recompute with your own price deck. Script: `scratchpad\scripts\lucius_calc.py`.
