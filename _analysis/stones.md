## 0. Bottom line

1. **FACT: The 0.015 ties out exactly on all 8 blocks.** Each block has two BP reservations, both stated "of 8/8ths":
   - 1.26725% reserved in the assignment to Shell (44%).
   - 0.23275% reserved in the assignment to Marathon (10%).
   - 1.26725% + 0.23275% = **1.50000%** = 0.015.

   The 16 instruments use one template. None has a depth limit, payout trigger, conversion right or "proportionately reduced" language.
2. **FACT: Only WR 508 (OCS-G 17001) receives production.** Its serial register says "Placed on continuous production on 09/02/2016". The other seven registers say "Held by unit production, lease not receiving an allocation" every month from Oct 2016 to May 2018.
   - **INFERENCE:** today the whole package's value comes from the WR 508 ORRI. Because the ORRI rate is the same 1.5% on all 8 leases, the effective share of unit production is still 0.015, however production is later allocated *among the 8 unit leases*.
3. **FACT: Wood Mackenzie puts the field over WR 464, 507 and 508.** WR 464 (G32690, Shell 100%) is **not in the unit and not in this package**.
   - **INFERENCE:** any part of WoodMac's gross field production that comes from WR 464 carries no ORRI. That is the main leakage risk to "gross × 0.015".
4. **FACT: The unit agreement has an automatic contraction clause (Art. XIII §13.1).** Any lease not receiving an allocation on the 5th anniversary of the initial participating area (PA) drops out of the unit.
   - **INFERENCE:** if the PA started at first production (about Sep 2016), that date is about Sep 2021. The 7 non-allocated leases are long past their primary terms, so unless the PA expands they would then expire. Their ORRIs would be extinguished.
   - **FACT:** the assignments contain **no anti-washout or renewal clause**. WR 594 and 595 show what happens: they dropped out of the unit, terminated on 9/30/2009, and Shell re-leased them in 2017 as G36087 and G36088 (WoodMac). **INFERENCE:** Shell took the new leases free of any old BP ORRI.
   - Upside on the 7 non-producing blocks is therefore option value only. Treat it as near zero.
5. **INFERENCE (base value):** PV10 of 1.5% of WoodMac's gross revenue is about **$105.6M at 1/1/2018**, discounted mid-year. This is before transportation allowances and before any WR 464 leakage.
   - **FACT:** WoodMac's "Rem PV 7,043.1" is the PV of **gross revenue**, not NPV. I reproduced it exactly: 0.015 × 7,043.1 = $105.6M.
   - Sensitivities are in §6.

## 7. Red flags and diligence requests
1. **The 7 of 8 blocks are non-producing:** they are "not receiving an allocation". There is a §13.1 automatic contraction around Sep 2021 and no anti-washout clause. Value only the WR 508 cash flow; treat the other 7 as option value.
2. **WR 464 sits in WoodMac's field outline but outside the unit and the package.** This is a possible overstatement if gross field volumes are used without adjustment. Ask for Exhibit C (PA schedule) and the allocation history from Shell.
3. **WR 551 BP→Shell assignment copy is incomplete** (page "2" missing; the ORRI clause is truncated). Obtain a certified complete copy.
4. **The Apr 26, 2006 BP/Shell/Marathon Letter Agreement and the Nov 29, 2004 JOA control over the assignments,** but neither is provided.
5. **Confirm the 0.015 decimal against BP's ORRI payment history since 9/2016.** BP should have about 20 months of checks by the bid date.
6. **WoodMac numbers vs narrative mismatch** (peak 68 vs 47 kboe/d; year 2019 vs 2020). Size the bid off a downside case.
7. **File name and date typos** (OSG-G, 5-01-08, WR 597 expiration) are cosmetic. The underlying documents match.
